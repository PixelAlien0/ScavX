Mod Changelog

Complete revision history of ScavX balance adjustments, new super-units, weapon mechanics, and lobby command restructuring.

ScavX v2.5 LATEST RELEASE Current Revision 8-PART BATCH

Major balance & titan expansion introducing the Titaniator super-heavy walker, doubling health pools for key boss units, tuning super-turrets, and expanding the lobby command chain to 8 modular parts.

⚡ New Unit: Titaniator
  • Heavy Titan v2: Arm Bantha variant boasting 1,000,000 HP with high mobility (51.6 speed).
  • Teh Lazer of Dewm: Piercing high-intensity laser dealing 15,000 damage/shot (5.5s reload).
  • Rapid Fire & Rockets: Equipped with 3,000 dmg bantha plasma fire + 1,500 dmg rocket salvos.
  • Scav Wave Integration: 20-unit Raider squad AI wave (anger 90–100).
👑 Boss & Titan Buffs
  • Queen Tyrannus: HP raised from 1.507M to 1,907,000 HP (+400k HP). Dual leveller cannons buffed to 25,000 dmg, dual heatrays to 3,000 dmg, AA missiles to 2,000 dmg.
  • Multiplicable: Base HP doubled from 1.003M to 2,003,000 HP. Anger threshold min increased to 80.
  • Walking Fortress: Base HP doubled from 1.0M to 2,000,000 HP for long-range 550 AoE siege survival.
  • King Chimera: Boss cannon damage increased from 5,200 to 10,200 per shell (10-shot burst = 102,000 dmg).
  • Nukinator: Tactical ICBM missile damage buffed from 21,500 to 24,500 dmg (320 AoE).
🛡️ Defense & Turret Limits
  • Ground Decimator: Damage normalized to 60,000 default / 40,000 shields. Hard-capped to 16 units max per player to prevent engine lag.
  • Epic Xyston: Adv SAM damage rebalanced to 40,000 ground / 80,000 vs VTOL air.
  • Epic Mini Ragnarok: Damage adjusted to 7,000 (0.5s reload). Metal cost set to 25k, energy to 450k.
  • Sanctuary of All Sanctuary: Boosted metal production to +1,000 metal/sec. Capped at 6 units max.
  • Fountain Cannon: Hard-capped to 4 units max per player.
  • Epic Wall: Hard-capped to 500 units max per player.
⚙️ 8-Part Modular Injections
  • Expanded to 8 Commands: Added !bset tweakdefs6 to accommodate expanded titan and defense definitions.
  • Chat Buffer Safety: Prevents lobby payload truncation and syntax drops in Beyond All Reason battle rooms.
  • Squad AI Adjustments: Legion Fig Def squad count updated to 25. Custom anger windows tuned for mid/late game balance.

Instructions

Host Setup Guide

How to apply the tweaks when hosting in Beyond All Reason.

01

Create Custom Lobby

In the Beyond All Reason client, select Multiplayer > Host Custom Game. Set game type to Scavengers.

02

Paste !bset Strings

Copy each of the 8 commands from the Tweak Commands section above into the battle room chat window.

03

Verify Constructors

In game, select an Advanced Construction Unit or Commander. Check the build menu for custom entries like Epic Bastion, Ground Decimator, and T3 Builder.
